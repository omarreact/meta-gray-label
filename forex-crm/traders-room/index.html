Give clients one branded place to open accounts, submit KYC documents, fund trading accounts, request withdrawals and manage their profile.

The trader’s room is designed to sit between your CRM, payment providers and MetaTrader environment so routine client actions do not depend on manual back-office work.

Account management

Clients can view and manage trading accounts, balances and profile details from a branded portal.

02

Funding workflow

Deposits and withdrawal requests can be connected to your payment and back-office approval workflow.

KYC visibility

Document status and onboarding progress stay visible to both clients and authorized staff.
