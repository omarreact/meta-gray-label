MT4 Grey Label for Brokers Who Want Control on a Platform Traders Trust

MT4 has outlasted a lot of predictions. Every few years someone says it’s on the way out, and every year millions of traders keep opening it each morning. Their indicators are on it, their EAs are on it, and they really don’t want to start over somewhere else.

For a broker, that loyalty is useful. An MT4 grey label lets you offer the platform those traders already know, under your own brand, with the manager-level control to run your business properly. You get the independence of a serious brokerage without paying for a full MT4 server licence.

01

What an MT4 Grey Label Includes

Your brokerage runs on an MT4 server that’s already licensed, hosted and maintained by us. On top of that, you get your own manager and admin access, your own account groups and the freedom to set your trading conditions.

In short, the server and its upkeep are our job. How your brokerage runs on it is yours.

Manager Terminal Access

Your dealing team can see open positions, monitor exposure, manage client accounts and adjust settings in real time. No waiting on someone else to make a change.

Your Own Groups and Conditions

Create groups for different client types, regions or account tiers. Set spreads, commissions, swaps and leverage for each one. If a new strategy needs a new group, you set it up yourself.

Full Branding

The terminal, client portal and communications all carry your name. Traders see your brokerage, nobody else’s.

02

Why Run as a Grey Label MT4 Broker?

Operating as a grey label MT4 broker puts you in a strong middle position. You’re not limited the way white label brokers often are, and you’re not carrying the huge cost and technical weight of a full licence.

Keep Your Existing Traders Happy

If you’re an introducing broker or educator with an MT4 audience, moving them to a new platform is a real risk. Some will follow you. Some won’t. Staying on MT4 removes that friction completely.

Run Your Own Risk Strategy

With a grey label, you decide how client flow is handled. Route some to liquidity providers, keep some in-house, treat different groups differently. That flexibility directly affects how much your brokerage can earn.

Move Faster Than Your Competitors

When conditions change, you can adjust spreads or leverage the same day. Brokers on restricted setups often have to request changes and wait, which costs them clients during volatile periods.

What Does MT4 Grey Label Cost?

The MT4 grey label cost sits above a white label and well below a full licence. Exactly where it lands depends on what you need, so here’s what shapes the final figure.

Factors That Affect the Price

The number of client accounts you expect. How many groups and custom symbols you need. Whether you want MT4 alone or MT4 alongside MT5. Which add-ons you take, like CRM, website or extra payment gateways. And the level of support you’d like.

Setup Fee and Monthly Fee

You’ll pay a one-time setup fee covering configuration, branding, integrations and testing, plus a monthly fee for server access, maintenance and support. We list both clearly in your proposal.

No Hidden Extras

Routine configuration changes are part of your monthly fee. If you ever need something beyond your package, we quote it first and you decide. Nothing shows up on an invoice that you haven’t agreed to.

05

Who an MT4 Grey Label Suits

It’s a good fit for white label brokers who’ve outgrown their restrictions, IBs with strong MT4 volumes ready to run their own book, and established firms launching a forex-focused brand. If most of your traders are forex specialists who rely on MT4 tools, it’s usually the most practical choice.

If you’re planning to expand heavily into stocks, indices or futures, we may suggest MT5 alongside it. We’ll talk that through with you openly.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can my traders use their existing MT4 indicators and EAs?

Yes. Because it’s standard MT4, traders can bring their custom indicators, scripts and expert advisors with them. That’s one of the biggest reasons brokers choose MT4 for their grey label.

Can I add MT5 later?

Absolutely. Many brokers start on MT4 and add MT5 once they want to offer more asset classes. We can run both side by side so clients choose the platform they prefer.

How quickly can an MT4 grey label go live?

Once your documents, branding and trading conditions are agreed, a standard setup can be live within a few days. Extra integrations or complex group structures can add a little time, and we’ll tell you upfront if they will.

Do I need my own dealing team?

It helps. A grey label gives you real control over risk and conditions, and you’ll get the most from it with someone managing that side. If you don’t have a team yet, we can talk through whether a white label is a better first step.
