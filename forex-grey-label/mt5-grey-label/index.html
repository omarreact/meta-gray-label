Today’s traders rarely stick to one market. The same client might trade EUR/USD before breakfast, gold at lunch and the Nasdaq in the evening. If your brokerage can only offer part of that, they’ll quietly open an account somewhere else.

An MT5 grey label gives you a platform that handles all of it, under your own brand, with the manager access and control to run your brokerage on your own terms. You skip the cost of a full MT5 server licence but keep the independence that serious brokers need.

01

What You Get With an MT5 Grey Label

Your brokerage runs on an MT5 server that’s already licensed, hosted and kept up to date by us. You get your own manager and admin access, your own groups and symbols, and the freedom to set trading conditions the way you want.

We look after the infrastructure. You make the business decisions.

A Branded Platform Across Every Device

Your traders log in to a platform carrying your name on desktop, web, iOS and Android. From their side, it’s simply your brokerage.

Control From the Manager Terminal

Your team can track positions, watch exposure, manage accounts and change settings in real time. When markets move fast, you don’t need to wait for anyone’s approval.

02

Why Become a Grey Label MT5 Broker?

Setting up as a grey label MT5 broker gives you two big advantages at once. A platform designed for multi-asset trading, and the control to make the most of it.

Offer More Than Forex

MT5 supports forex, metals, energies, indices, stocks, futures and crypto CFDs in one terminal. You can start with a focused product list and expand when your clients ask for more, without changing platforms or moving accounts.

Set Conditions for Each Asset Class

Different instruments need different treatment. With a grey label, you can set separate spreads, commissions, leverage and trading hours by symbol and by group. Your index traders and forex scalpers don’t have to share the same rules.

Manage Your Own Risk

Decide how flow is handled across asset classes. Route some to liquidity providers, keep some internally, and adjust as your book changes. For a multi-asset broker, that flexibility matters even more than it does for a pure forex shop.

Why MT5 Is the Smart Choice for New Grey Label Brokers

MT4 still has its fans, and we support it too. But most brokers launching a grey label today choose MT5, and for good reasons.

Built for Where Trading Is Heading

MetaQuotes focuses its development on MT5. New features, integrations and plugins arrive there first. Starting on MT5 keeps your brokerage current for years rather than months.

Better Tools for Active and Algo Traders

More timeframes, more order types, market depth, a built-in economic calendar and a much faster multi-threaded strategy tester. Experienced traders pick up on these quickly and appreciate them.

Hedging and Netting Modes

MT5 supports both hedging and netting account types. That gives you flexibility to serve traditional forex traders as well as those used to exchange-style position handling.

Understanding MT5 Grey Label Cost

The MT5 grey label cost sits comfortably between a white label and a full licence. The exact figure depends on the size and shape of your setup.

What Shapes the Price

Expected client numbers. How many asset classes and symbols you want to offer. Whether you need MT5 alone or alongside MT4. Which add-ons you include, such as CRM, payments and website. And the support level you choose.

How Fees Are Structured

A one-time setup fee covers configuration, branding, integrations and testing. A monthly fee covers server access, maintenance and support. Both are listed clearly in your proposal.

Pricing Without Surprises

Routine changes are covered in your monthly fee. Anything outside your package gets quoted before work starts, and you decide whether to go ahead. No unexpected lines on your invoice.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I offer stocks and crypto CFDs on an MT5 grey label?

Yes. MT5 handles stocks, indices, commodities, futures and crypto CFDs alongside forex. What you can offer also depends on your liquidity providers and licensing, which we can talk through during setup.

Will traders used to MT4 struggle with MT5?

Most adjust within a day or two, as the layout is similar. The main hurdle is custom MT4 indicators and EAs, which need MQL5 versions. If that’s a concern, we can run MT4 alongside MT5.

How long does an MT5 grey label take to launch?

A standard setup can go live within a few days once your documents, branding and trading conditions are agreed. Multi-asset configurations with many symbols may need a little more time, and we’ll let you know upfront.

Can I switch from an MT5 white label to a grey label?

Yes. Since both run on our infrastructure, we can move your groups and client accounts across with minimal disruption. Many brokers make this step once their volumes grow.
