Common launch questions

Understand the model before you configure the stack.

The right answer depends on target markets, platform preference, operating model and how much control your team needs.

MT4 or MT5: which should I choose?

MT5 supports a broader multi-asset architecture, while MT4 remains familiar to a large trading audience. The correct platform depends on your products, client expectations and operating plan.

What does a grey label provide?

A branded MetaTrader environment with manager access and more control over trading groups, spreads and conditions than a typical white-label arrangement.

Can I move to a different model later?

Yes. A brokerage can plan a transition from white label to grey label or expand toward a broader turnkey stack as operational needs change.

What is included in a turnkey setup?

A turnkey path can coordinate the trading platform, CRM, client portal, liquidity connections, payment integration, website and launch support.

How long does setup take?

A two-day platform setup may be possible when company documents, platform requirements and target markets are already confirmed. Licensing, payments and external approvals can take longer.

What should we prepare first?

Confirm your company documents, target markets, platform preference, intended launch window and the level of infrastructure control you want to operate.
