Trader’s Room and Client Portal Features in a MetaTrader Grey Label Setup

Practical perspectives on brokerage technology and operations.

6 min read · Meta Grey Label

The context

Your traders spend most of their time in MetaTrader. But almost everything else they do with your brokerage happens somewhere else: signing up, verifying their identity, funding an account, moving money between accounts, asking for help, withdrawing profits. All of that happens in the client portal, often called the trader’s room.

It’s easy to treat the portal as an afterthought, something you sort out once the server is running. That’s a mistake. For many clients, the portal is your brokerage. It’s the first thing they use and the place they come back to every time money is involved.

Here’s what a strong forex client portal should include, and how it fits into a grey label setup.

01 / Explore the detail

Why the Portal Matters So Much

Think about it from the trader’s side. They’ve probably used a few brokers before. They know what a smooth sign-up feels like, and what it feels like to wait three days for a withdrawal with no updates.

A good portal makes your brokerage feel organised and trustworthy. A clumsy one makes even excellent execution look suspect. Traders reasonably assume that if the basics are messy, the rest might be too.

With a grey label, you run your own MetaTrader server, so your portal can connect to it directly. That makes it possible to build a far smoother experience than many white label setups allow.

02 / Explore the detail

Registration and Onboarding

The first few minutes count most. Good trader’s room software keeps registration short and gets clients to a funded account as quickly as your compliance rules allow.

01

Features to Look For

A short sign-up form, with extra details collected later rather than all at once

Social or email sign-up options where your regulator allows them

Mobile-friendly design, since many clients sign up from their phones

Clear progress steps so clients know what’s left to do

Language options for the markets you serve

02

KYC and Verification

Clients should be able to upload ID and proof of address straight from their phone camera. Automated checks can approve straightforward cases quickly and send unclear ones to your compliance team. The portal should also show clients exactly where their verification stands, so they aren’t left guessing or emailing support.

03 / Explore the detail

Account Management

Once verified, traders want control over their own accounts without having to contact anyone.

A good portal lets them:

Open new live or demo accounts instantly

Choose from the account types you offer, such as standard, raw spread or swap-free

Change leverage within the limits you’ve set

Reset MetaTrader passwords

See all their accounts, balances and equity in one place

Because a grey label gives you admin control over your server, these actions can sync to MetaTrader immediately. A new account opened in the portal is ready to trade within moments.

04 / Explore the detail

Deposits, Withdrawals and Transfers

This is where the portal earns its keep, and where most complaints start if things go wrong.

01

Deposits

Offer the payment methods your target markets actually use: cards, bank transfer, local e-wallets, and crypto where permitted. Deposits should show up in the client’s MetaTrader balance automatically once payment is confirmed. Every extra step between paying and seeing funds is a chance for a client to lose patience.

02

Withdrawals

Clients should be able to request a withdrawal in a few clicks, see its status clearly, and get notified at each stage. On your side, the back office should flag requests that need extra checks while letting routine ones go through quickly.

03

Internal Transfers

Traders with multiple accounts often want to move funds between them. Instant internal transfers are a small feature that clients really appreciate.

05 / Explore the detail

Trading Insights and Reports

Many traders like to see their performance outside MetaTrader. A portal can show:

Trade history and account statements

Profit and loss over custom periods

Deposit and withdrawal history

Downloadable reports for tax or personal records

Some brokers go further with simple analytics, such as win rates, average trade duration or most-traded instruments. It’s not essential, but it’s the kind of detail that makes a portal feel thoughtful.

06 / Explore the detail

Support and Communication

The portal is a natural home for support. Look for:

A ticket system where clients can follow their requests

Live chat linked to the client’s account details, so agents don’t have to ask for login numbers

A notification centre for important updates, such as margin warnings, new account types or scheduled maintenance

An FAQ or help section covering the questions your team gets asked again and again

Good support tools cut your workload too. Clients who can find answers themselves raise fewer tickets.

07 / Explore the detail

Features That Add Extra Value

Once the essentials are solid, extra features can set your portal apart.

01

Copy Trading and PAMM Access

If you offer copy trading or managed accounts, the portal is where clients browse strategy providers, check performance and choose who to follow. Investors should be able to see their allocations and results without logging into separate systems.

02

IB and Affiliate Areas

Introducing brokers need their own section to see their referred clients, trading volumes and commissions. A clear, accurate IB area helps you attract and keep good partners.

03

Education and Market Content

Some brokers put tutorials, market analysis, economic calendars or webinar sign-ups in the portal. It keeps clients coming back and gives newer traders a reason to stay with you as they learn.

08 / Explore the detail

What Your Team Needs Behind the Scenes

The client side gets the attention, but the back office matters just as much. Your team should be able to:

View a client’s full profile, accounts and trading activity in one place

Approve KYC, deposits and withdrawals with a clear audit trail

Move clients between groups or change conditions when needed

Pull reports for compliance and management

Set permissions so each team member only sees what they need

When your forex client portal and back office share data properly with your grey label server, your team spends less time chasing information and more time helping clients.

09 / Explore the detail

Choosing the Right Software

When comparing trader’s room software, a few questions sort the strong options from the weak:

Does it integrate directly with your MetaTrader grey label server in real time?

Can it be fully branded, including domain, colours and emails?

How well does it work on mobile?

Which payment providers does it already support?

Can it grow with you to cover copy trading, PAMM and IB programmes?

How are updates and new features handled?

Ask for a live demo connected to a test server. Go through it as a new client would, from sign-up to first withdrawal. You’ll spot problems that no feature list will tell you about.

10 / Explore the detail

Conclusion

Your trading server does the heavy lifting, but your client portal shapes how traders feel about your brokerage. Get registration, payments and account management right, and everything else becomes easier.
