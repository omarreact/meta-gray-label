Explore SolutionsAll Services

Watch how most people trade now and you’ll notice something. The phone comes out first. They check positions over breakfast, react to news on the train, close a trade in a queue at the supermarket. For a growing share of traders, the phone isn’t a backup. It’s the main way they trade.

A reliable mobile trading app for brokers isn’t a nice extra anymore. It’s expected. If your clients can’t trade comfortably on their phones, they’ll find a broker where they can.

01

Why Mobile Matters So Much

Markets Don’t Wait

Prices move at all hours. A trader who can only act from a desktop misses opportunities and can’t protect positions when something unexpected happens.

Clients Check In Constantly

Even traders who do their analysis on a desktop tend to monitor positions on their phone throughout the day. That’s often when they decide to close, adjust or add to a trade.

New Traders Start on Mobile

Many newer, younger traders have never used a desktop trading terminal at all. For them, the app is your brokerage.

02

MT4 MT5 Mobile App Features

The MT4 MT5 mobile app gives your clients a full trading experience in their pocket, connected to the same accounts they use on desktop and web.

Live Prices and Charts

Real-time quotes, interactive charts with multiple timeframes, and a range of built-in technical indicators and drawing tools.

Full Order Management

Open and close positions, place pending orders, and set or adjust stop loss and take profit levels in a few taps.

Account Overview

Balance, equity, margin, free margin and open positions, all visible at a glance.

Trade History

Clients can review past trades and account activity whenever they need to.

Push Notifications

Price alerts and trade notifications keep clients informed even when the app is closed.

Financial News

Built-in news feeds help traders stay on top of market events without switching apps.

03

MT4 Mobile vs MT5 Mobile

MT4 Mobile App

Ideal for forex-focused traders who already use MT4. It’s simple, fast and handles core trading tasks well.

MT5 Mobile App

Better suited to multi-asset brokerages. It supports more instruments, more timeframes and more order types, and offers a slightly richer charting experience.

Offering Both

If you run both platforms, clients can download either app and log in with the matching account.

04

iOS Android Trading App Coverage

A proper iOS Android trading app setup means none of your clients are left out, whatever phone they use.

iPhone and iPad

Clients on Apple devices download MetaTrader from the App Store and connect to your server.

Android Phones and Tablets

Android users download from Google Play and connect the same way.

One Login Everywhere

Clients use the same account details on mobile as they do on desktop and web. Positions and balances stay in sync across every device.

How Clients Find Your Brokerage in the App

This part often confuses new brokers, so it’s worth explaining clearly.

The Standard MetaTrader App

Most brokers’ clients use the official MetaTrader app, published by MetaQuotes. After downloading it, clients search for your server name and log in with their account details. Your server name and company appear inside the app.

Making It Easy for Clients

We set up your server name clearly and help you create simple step-by-step guides, direct download links and QR codes for your website and trader’s room. That way clients connect to the right server first time.

White Label Forex Trading App Options

Some brokers want a fully branded app with their own name and icon in the app stores. A white label forex trading app like this is possible, but it depends on your licence level and setup.

When It’s Available

Brokers holding their own MetaTrader licence, or working with certain third-party app providers, may be able to publish a branded mobile app. Availability, cost and timelines vary.

Is It Worth It?

A branded app can strengthen your brand and make marketing easier. But for many new brokers, the standard MetaTrader app with clear onboarding works perfectly well. We’ll give you an honest view of which makes sense for your stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do clients need a separate account to trade on mobile?

No. They use the same login details as on desktop and web, and everything stays in sync.

Can I have my own branded app in the App Store and Google Play?

Possibly, depending on your licence and setup. Many brokers start with the standard MetaTrader app and consider a branded app as they grow.

Can clients use expert advisors on mobile?

No. Expert advisors and most custom indicators run on the desktop terminal. The mobile app is built for manual trading, monitoring and quick decisions.

Does the mobile app work on tablets?

Yes. MetaTrader runs on iPads and Android tablets as well as phones.
