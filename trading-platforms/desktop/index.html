Mobile and web trading are convenient, but when traders want to do real work, they sit down at a desktop. That’s where they run multiple charts side by side, build and test strategies, leave expert advisors trading overnight and dig into the detail behind every decision.

For your most active and valuable clients, the desktop platform is where they live. Offering a reliable MT4 desktop terminal and MT5 desktop terminal, branded with your name, tells those traders your brokerage is built for them.

01

Why the Desktop Platform Still Matters

Your Most Active Traders Use It

Clients who trade often, trade larger volumes or run automated strategies almost always rely on the desktop terminal. They tend to generate a big share of a brokerage’s trading activity.

The Full Toolkit Lives Here

Custom indicators, expert advisors, scripts, the strategy tester and advanced chart layouts all work best, and in many cases only work, on desktop.

Bigger Screen, Better Decisions

Multiple monitors, detailed charts and several instruments open at once. Desktop gives traders the space to analyse properly.

02

MT4 Desktop Terminal

The MT4 desktop terminal remains one of the most widely used trading tools in the world, especially among forex traders.

What Traders Get

Nine timeframes, a wide range of built-in indicators and drawing tools, one-click trading, multiple chart windows and full support for custom indicators and expert advisors written in MQL4.

Why Traders Stay Loyal

Many traders have spent years building or buying MT4 tools. Their entire trading routine depends on them. Offering MT4 on desktop lets these clients move to your brokerage without giving anything up.

Best Suited For

Forex-focused traders, long-time MT4 users and those who rely on established EAs and indicators.

03

MT5 Desktop Terminal

The MT5 desktop terminal builds on everything MT4 offers and adds more depth for modern, multi-asset trading.

What Traders Get

Twenty-one timeframes, more built-in indicators and analytical objects, additional order types, market depth on supported instruments, a built-in economic calendar and support for MQL5 expert advisors and indicators.

A Faster Strategy Tester

MT5’s multi-threaded strategy tester can test EAs across multiple currencies and use real tick data, making it far quicker and more accurate than MT4’s. Algo traders notice this straight away.

Multi-Asset Trading

Forex, metals, energies, indices, stocks, futures and crypto CFDs can all be traded from one terminal, depending on what your brokerage offers.

Best Suited For

Multi-asset traders, algorithmic traders and newer clients who want the most current MetaTrader experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the desktop terminal work on Mac?

MetaTrader is built for Windows, but Mac users can run it using available Mac versions or compatibility tools. We’ll help you provide clear instructions for Mac clients.

Can traders use the same account on desktop, web and mobile?

Yes. One set of login details works across all devices, and everything stays in sync.

Can clients run MT4 EAs on MT5?

Not directly. MT4 expert advisors are written in MQL4 and need to be rewritten in MQL5 to run on MT5. If many of your clients rely on MT4 tools, offering both platforms is often the easiest solution.

Will the desktop platform carry my branding?

Yes, to the extent your setup allows. We’ll explain exactly what branding is available with your white label, grey label or admin licence.
