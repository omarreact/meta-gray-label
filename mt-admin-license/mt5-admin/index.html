MT5 Admin License for Brokers Ready to Own Their Trading Infrastructure

MT5 has become the platform most new brokerages build on. It handles forex, metals, indices, stocks, futures and crypto CFDs in one place, and it’s where…

Explore SolutionsAll Services

MT5 has become the platform most new brokerages build on. It handles forex, metals, indices, stocks, futures and crypto CFDs in one place, and it’s where MetaQuotes now puts almost all of its development effort. For brokers planning to grow, owning that platform outright is a serious step forward.

An MT5 admin license gives your company its own MetaTrader 5 server, licensed in your name, with full administrator access. No provider sitting above you, no limits on how you configure things. This page explains what you get, what it costs, how setup works and whether the timing is right for you.

01

What an MT5 Admin License Gives You

With your own licence, the server and everything running on it belong to your brokerage. You make the decisions, your team holds the keys.

Full Administrator Access

Server settings, plugins, gateways, data feeds, backups and security are all under your control. Your team can act immediately when something needs changing, rather than raising a request and waiting.

Freedom to Build Your Product

Choose which asset classes to offer, how each symbol is configured and how trading sessions run. Set spreads, commissions, swaps and leverage by group, by region or by instrument. Your offer can be as simple or as detailed as your business needs.

Ownership of Your Data

Client records, trading history and server logs sit on infrastructure your company controls. That makes regulatory reporting, audits and data protection much easier to manage.

02

Why MT5 Is the Licence Most Brokers Choose Today

Unlike MT4, an MT5 server license is still actively issued and supported by MetaQuotes, which makes it the practical choice for most brokers moving to their own infrastructure.

True Multi-Asset Trading

One server can carry forex, metals, energies, indices, stocks, futures and crypto CFDs. As your clients ask for new markets, you add symbols rather than new platforms.

Hedging and Netting Support

MT5 lets you run both hedging and netting account types. That means you can serve traditional forex traders and those used to exchange-style position handling from the same server.

Better Performance and Tools

The multi-threaded strategy tester, more timeframes, more order types, market depth and a built-in economic calendar all make MT5 attractive to active and algorithmic traders.

A Platform That Keeps Improving

New features, gateways and integrations land on MT5 first. Owning an MT5 server means your brokerage stays current without a painful migration down the line.

03

Understanding MT5 Admin Cost

The MT5 admin cost isn’t a single number. It’s a combination of several parts, and knowing each one helps you budget properly.

Licence Fees

The licence itself is issued by MetaQuotes, and its fees are the largest upfront cost. The exact amount depends on the licence package and the arrangement agreed.

Hosting and Infrastructure

Your MT5 server needs reliable hosting, ideally in a data centre close to your liquidity providers to keep latency low. Costs depend on location, performance needs and backup requirements.

Integrations

A liquidity bridge, forex CRM, client portal, payment gateways and risk management tools all add to your budget. If you already use some of these, we can connect them rather than replace them.

Server Management

Someone has to keep the server running smoothly. That could be your in-house team, a managed service from us, or both. Monthly costs depend on the level of support you choose.

Getting a Real Number

Every setup is different, so the best way to understand your MT5 admin cost is to tell us your plans. We’ll send an itemised estimate with every part explained.

MT5 Admin Setup: Our Step-by-Step Process

A careful MT5 admin setup is what separates a stable launch from weeks of firefighting. Here’s how we approach it.

Step One: Understand Your Business

We look at where you are now. Are you starting fresh, upgrading from a grey label, or moving from MT4? Which markets and asset classes are you targeting? That shapes everything else.

Step Two: Licensing Support

We guide you through the MetaQuotes licensing process, the documents involved and what to expect at each stage.

Step Three: Server and Hosting

We help you choose hosting, then build and configure your MT5 environment for speed, stability and security.

Step Four: Configuration

Groups, symbols, trading sessions, account types and plugins are set up around your business model. If you’re migrating, we replicate what works and fix what doesn’t.

Step Five: Integrations and Testing

Your CRM, client portal, payments and liquidity bridge are connected and tested together. Your team runs through real trading and client scenarios before launch.

Step Six: Go Live and Ongoing Support

You launch with full administrator access. Afterwards, we can manage the server completely, support your team, or step in only when needed.

Who Should Get an MT5 Admin License?

It’s a strong fit for grey label brokers whose volumes now justify their own server, multi-asset brokers who need deep control over symbols and sessions, firms entering regulated markets where owning infrastructure matters, and MT4 brokers ready to move to a modern platform.

If you’re still testing a market or building your first client base, a white label or grey label is often the smarter first step. We’ll give you an honest view of which suits you.

06

Own the Platform Your Brokerage Runs On

An MT5 admin license gives you the control, credibility and flexibility that shared setups can’t offer. It’s a big step, and it deserves careful planning and honest advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get an MT5 admin licence?

The licensing review usually takes a few weeks, depending on your documents. Server setup and integrations run alongside it, so you can often launch soon after approval.

Can I move my grey label clients to my own MT5 server?

Yes. We migrate accounts, balances and trading history, usually over a weekend when markets are closed, so your traders face minimal disruption.

Can I keep MT4 running while I move to MT5?

Yes. Some brokers keep MT4 for existing clients while onboarding new traders to MT5. We can set up and manage both during the transition, or for as long as you need.

Do I need my own technical team?

You’ll need someone responsible for server administration. That can be your own staff, our managed service, or a mix. We’ll help you decide what makes sense for your size and budget.
