The context

Traders want more than currency pairs these days. They want gold in the morning, US indices in the afternoon and maybe a few stocks or crypto CFDs on the side. If your platform can’t keep up, they’ll find one that can.

MetaTrader 5 was built for that kind of trading. With an MT5 white label, you get the full platform under your own brand, ready for clients across multiple asset classes, without paying for a server licence or building infrastructure from scratch.

What You Get With an MT5 White Label

You run your brokerage on an MT5 server that’s already licensed, hosted and kept up to date. We brand the platform with your name and logo, set up your account groups and symbols, and connect it to a back office so you can start onboarding clients quickly.

Your traders see your brand on desktop, web and mobile. Everything underneath is managed for you.

01

Multi-Asset From the Start

MT5 handles forex, metals, energies, indices, stocks and futures in one terminal. You can launch with a tight product list and add more instruments as your clients ask for them, without changing platforms.

02

A Platform Built for Modern Traders

More timeframes, more order types, a built-in economic calendar, market depth and a far stronger strategy tester. Active traders and algo traders notice the difference quickly.

Who Should Become a White Label MT5 Forex Broker?

Launching as a white label MT5 forex broker works especially well if you’re planning to offer more than forex, or if your audience is younger and less tied to older tools.

It suits introducing brokers expanding into their own brand, fintech and investment firms that want CFDs alongside existing products, trading communities with followers interested in indices and commodities, and new founders who want a platform that won’t feel dated in a few years.

If your plans include stocks, crypto CFDs or futures at any point, starting on MT5 saves you a migration later.

MT5 vs MT4: Why More Brokers Are Choosing MT5

MT4 still has a loyal following, and we support it. But most new brokerages we work with now start on MT5, and the reasons are fairly practical.

01

Wider Range of Instruments

MT4 was designed mainly for forex. MT5 handles exchange-traded instruments and a much broader mix of CFDs, which gives you more to offer and more ways to earn.

02

Better Tools for Algo Traders

The MT5 strategy tester is multi-threaded and much faster, and MQL5 is a more powerful language than its predecessor. Traders building and testing EAs tend to prefer it once they’ve tried it.

03

Where the Industry Is Heading

MetaQuotes has focused its development on MT5 for years. New features, integrations and plugins land there first. Choosing MT5 now keeps your brokerage aligned with where the platform ecosystem is going.

04

You Can Still Offer Both

If part of your audience is attached to MT4, we can run both platforms side by side. Clients pick what they’re comfortable with, and you don’t lose anyone over a platform preference.

MT5 White Label Turnkey: The Whole Brokerage, Ready to Go

A branded platform is a strong start, but clients also need to register, pass KYC, deposit funds and reach support. An MT5 white label turnkey package brings all of that together in one setup.

01

What’s Included

Depending on what you need, the package can cover a branded MT5 platform across desktop, web, iOS and Android, a forex CRM with onboarding and KYC, a trader’s room for deposits, withdrawals and account management, payment gateway integrations, an IB and affiliate module, and a branded website. Optional extras like copy trading, PAMM and MAM accounts can be added as you grow.

02

One Team Responsible for All of It

Every piece is configured and tested together before launch. If something goes wrong later, there’s no chain of vendors pointing at each other. You call us, and we fix it.

What Does MT5 White Label Cost?

We get asked this on almost every first call. The real answer is that MT5 white label cost depends on how much you need. A standalone branded platform sits at one end. A full turnkey brokerage with CRM, payments and website sits at the other.

01

The Main Cost Factors

The biggest things that shape your price are the number of client accounts you expect, how many asset classes and symbols you want to offer, whether you need MT5 alone or alongside MT4, how much of the turnkey package you take, and which plugins and support level you choose.

02

How Fees Are Usually Structured

Typically there’s a one-time setup fee for configuration, branding and integrations, plus a monthly fee for server access, maintenance and support. Our proposal shows both clearly, line by line.

03

Clear Pricing, No Late Surprises

We don’t believe in a low headline price followed by extra charges for reports, plugins or support time. You’ll see the full picture before you sign. If anything changes down the line, we’ll talk to you first.
