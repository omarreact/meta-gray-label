The context

Every broker starts somewhere. Some begin on a white label, others on a grey label, and a few go straight for the real thing. Whichever route you take, there’s a point where owning your trading infrastructure becomes the logical choice.

An admin licence means the MetaTrader server is yours. Your company holds the licence, your team has full administrator access and every decision about how the platform runs sits with you. This page explains what that involves, who it suits and how we help you set it up properly.

01 / Operational scope

What Is an MT4 or MT5 Admin License?

It’s a full server licence for MetaTrader, issued in your company’s name. Unlike white or grey label arrangements, you aren’t working inside someone else’s environment. You own it.

With an MT4 admin license or MT5 admin license, you get administrator access to the server itself. That covers server settings, plugins, data feeds, gateways, backups, security and every group and symbol on the platform. Nothing needs approval from a provider above you.

01

Admin Access vs Manager Access

On a grey label, you work with manager access. That’s enough to run groups, trading conditions and client accounts. Admin access goes deeper. You control the server configuration, install plugins, connect any gateway you choose and decide how the infrastructure is hosted and maintained.

02 / Operational scope

Why Brokers Choose Their Own MetaTrader License

Holding your own MetaTrader license isn’t right for everyone. But for brokers at the right stage, it changes a lot.

01

Complete Independence

No provider limits on plugins, integrations or server settings. If you want to run custom risk tools, connect a specific liquidity bridge or build server-side logic, you can.

02

Stronger Credibility

Banks, payment providers, regulators and institutional clients often take a broker more seriously when it owns its infrastructure. It signals commitment and long-term stability.

03

Better Long-Term Economics

The upfront cost is higher, but there are no ongoing grey label fees tied to a provider’s server. For brokers with solid volumes, owning the licence often works out cheaper over a few years.

04

Full Control Over Your Data

Client data, trading history and server logs sit on infrastructure you control. That matters for compliance, audits and peace of mind.

03 / Operational scope

MT4 Admin License or MT5 Admin License?

Both give you full ownership. The right one depends on your traders and your product plans.

01

MT4 Admin License

Best for brokers with a forex-focused audience that relies on MT4 indicators and expert advisors. It’s familiar, stable and still widely used. One thing to note: new MT4 licences have become harder to obtain as MetaQuotes focuses on MT5, so availability is worth checking early.

02

MT5 Admin License

The natural choice for most new licence holders. MT5 handles forex, metals, indices, stocks, futures and crypto CFDs in one platform, supports both hedging and netting, and is where new features and integrations arrive first.

03

Running Both

Some brokers hold both licences so they can serve every type of trader. It adds cost and admin work, but it removes any platform barrier for new clients.

04 / Operational scope

What Comes With a MetaTrader License for Brokers

A MetaTrader license for brokers gives you the core server and platform. To turn it into a working brokerage, you’ll typically need several more pieces around it.

01

Core Components

The trading server, administrator and manager terminals, client terminals for desktop, web and mobile, and the data and history servers your platform runs on.

02

What You’ll Need Alongside It

Hosting in a reliable data centre, a liquidity bridge, a forex CRM and client portal, payment gateway integrations, risk management tools, backup and disaster recovery, and people who know how to keep it all running. We can help with every one of these.

06 / Operational scope

Is an Admin License Right for You Yet?

We’ll be straight with you. An admin licence is a big commitment. It suits brokers with steady volumes, a clear growth plan, and either an in-house technical team or a trusted partner to manage the server.

If you’re just starting out or still testing a market, a white label or grey label is usually the smarter first move. Many of our clients start there and move to their own licence once the numbers justify it. We’ll help you work out which stage you’re at.

07 / Operational scope

Take Full Ownership of Your Trading Platform

An MT4 admin license or MT5 admin license puts your brokerage on infrastructure that truly belongs to you. More control, more credibility and more room to build exactly the business you want.

Answers, without the ambiguity

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an MT4 or MT5 admin licence cost?

It depends on the licence type, your hosting choices, integrations and support level. Licence fees are set by MetaQuotes, and setup and management costs vary by provider. Share your plans with us and we’ll give you a clear, itemised estimate.

How long does it take to get my own MetaTrader licence?

The licensing process can take several weeks, depending on your documents and MetaQuotes’ review. Server setup and integrations usually run in parallel, so you can launch soon after approval.

Can I move my existing grey label clients to my own licence?

Yes. We can migrate client accounts, balances and trading history from a grey label to your new server, usually over a weekend, with minimal disruption to your traders.

Do I need a regulatory licence before applying?

Requirements vary. MetaQuotes may ask for company and regulatory documents during the process, and your target markets will have their own rules. Your legal advisor should confirm what applies to you.
