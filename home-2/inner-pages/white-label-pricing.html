The context

Pricing is usually the first thing brokers want to know and the last thing most providers explain clearly. You’ll often see “contact us for a quote” and nothing else. That makes it hard to plan a budget, let alone compare options.

So this page lays it out properly. What goes into a forex white label price, what you pay upfront, what you pay each month and what’s optional. By the end, you should have a good idea of where your setup is likely to land before you ever pick up the phone.

What Makes Up White Label Forex Cost?

Every brokerage is a little different, but the white label forex cost almost always breaks down into the same few parts.

01

The One-Time Setup Fee

This covers getting you live. Server configuration, branding the platform, creating your account groups and symbols, connecting your back office and testing everything before launch. You pay it once.

02

The Monthly Fee

This keeps everything running. Server access, hosting, platform updates, maintenance and support from our team. It’s a fixed amount each month, agreed upfront.

03

Optional Add-Ons

Extra tools you can bolt on when you need them. Copy trading, PAMM and MAM accounts, additional payment gateways, advanced risk management plugins and so on. Nothing here is forced on you at launch.

02 / Explore the detail

Understanding the White Label Setup Fee

The white label setup fee is the part people tend to worry about most, mainly because some providers use it to hide a lot of extra charges. Ours covers a clearly defined list of work.

01

What the Setup Fee Includes

Branding the trading terminal across desktop, web and mobile. Setting up your account groups, leverage, symbols and commission structure. Connecting the platform to your CRM and client portal. Integrating your chosen payment methods. Testing everything with your team before go live.

02

What Can Push It Higher

A few things add work and therefore cost. Running both MT4 and MT5 rather than one platform. A large number of custom symbols or complex group structures. Custom website design instead of a template. Integrations with third-party tools you already use.

We’ll flag any of these early, so the quote you approve is the quote you pay.

MT4 White Label Pricing Compared With MT5

Brokers often ask whether MT4 white label pricing differs from MT5. In most cases the gap is small, and the bigger cost difference comes from what you build around the platform rather than the platform itself.

01

When MT4 Makes Sense

If your audience is mainly forex traders who already use MT4 indicators and EAs, it’s a sensible choice and keeps things familiar for them.

02

When MT5 Makes Sense

If you plan to offer indices, commodities, stocks or crypto CFDs, MT5 handles all of it in one terminal. It also tends to be the better long-term pick for new brokerages.

03

Running Both

Offering both platforms adds some configuration work and a higher monthly fee, but it widens your audience. Plenty of brokers decide it’s worth it once they see how many traders care about platform choice.

Costs to Plan for Outside the White Label

Your technology is one part of the budget. To give you an honest picture, here are other costs brokers commonly need to plan for. These come from third parties, not from us.

Company registration and licensing, which vary widely by jurisdiction. Legal and compliance advice. Liquidity provider minimum deposits or fees. Payment processor setup and transaction fees. Marketing to bring in your first clients.

We can point you towards trusted partners for several of these, but we’d rather you know about them now than discover them later.

We work differently. Every cost in your setup is listed in the proposal before you sign. Routine configuration changes are part of your monthly fee. If you ever need something outside your package, we’ll quote it first and you decide.
