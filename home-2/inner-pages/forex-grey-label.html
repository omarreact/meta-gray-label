What Is a Forex Grey Label?

A grey label sits between a white label and a full MetaTrader licence. Your brokerage runs on a server that’s already licensed, hosted and maintained, but unlike a white label, you get your own manager access and much greater independence over how your business operates.

You decide your trading conditions. You build your own groups. Your team monitors exposure and manages clients directly. The heavy infrastructure work, like hosting, updates and server stability, stays with us.

01

Think of It Like This

A white label is like leasing a shop in a mall where management sets your opening hours and decides what you can stock. A grey label is more like leasing your own building. Someone else still owns the land and keeps the lights on, but inside, you run things your way.

What You Control as a Grey Label Forex Broker

Running as a grey label forex broker gives you a level of control that white label setups simply don’t offer.

01

Trading Conditions

Set your own spreads, markups, commissions, swaps and leverage. Adjust them by client group, account type or region. If you want a tighter offer for high-volume traders and a standard one for retail, you can do it.

02

Groups and Symbols

Create and manage your own account groups. Choose which instruments each group can trade and how they’re configured. Add new symbols as your product range grows.

03

Risk Management

This is where a grey label really earns its place. You decide how client flow is handled, how much goes to liquidity providers and how different groups are treated. For brokers serious about building revenue, that flexibility is hard to give up once you’ve had it.

04

Manager and Admin Terminals

Your dealing team gets proper manager terminal access. They can watch positions in real time, react to market moves and handle client issues without waiting on an outside team.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a grey label different from a white label?

Both let you trade under your own brand on an existing server. A grey label adds manager access and much greater control over trading conditions, groups and risk, while a white label keeps more of those settings under the main server’s oversight.

How long does a grey label setup take?

Once your documents and branding are ready, a standard setup can go live within a few days. More complex configurations, like running both MT4 and MT5 or adding several integrations, can take a little longer.

Can I move from a white label to a grey label later?

Yes, and many of our clients do exactly that. Because both run on our infrastructure, we move your groups and client accounts across with minimal disruption to your traders.

Do I need my own liquidity provider?

You’ll need liquidity connected to your setup, and we can integrate the providers you already work with. If you don’t have one yet, we can point you towards options that suit your size and markets.
