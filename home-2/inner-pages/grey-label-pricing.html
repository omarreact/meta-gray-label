The context

A grey label is a bigger step than a white label, so it makes sense that brokers want to understand the numbers properly before committing. The trouble is, most providers keep pricing vague until you’re deep into a sales call.

This page does the opposite. It explains what goes into a grey label forex price, how setup and monthly fees work, where the costs can rise and how to keep them under control. By the end, you should have a realistic sense of your budget before speaking to anyone.

Why a Grey Label Costs More Than a White Label

It’s a fair question. Both run on existing MetaTrader infrastructure, so why the price gap?

The answer is control. With a grey label, you get your own manager and admin access, independent groups, custom trading conditions and much more say over risk. That means more server resources, more configuration work and more hands-on support from our side. You’re paying for independence, and for most growing brokers, it pays for itself fairly quickly.

01

Still Far Below a Full Licence

To keep things in perspective, a grey label remains a fraction of what a full MetaTrader server licence costs once you add hosting, integrations and an in-house technical team. You get much of the freedom without that weight.

How Grey Label Pricing Is Structured

Your total cost comes down to three parts.

01

The Setup Fee

A one-time payment that covers getting you live. Server configuration, manager and admin access, branding, groups and symbols, liquidity bridge connection, CRM and payment integrations if included, and full testing with your team.

02

The Grey Label Monthly Fee

The grey label monthly fee covers keeping everything running. Server access and hosting, platform updates, routine configuration changes, monitoring and support from people who know MetaTrader properly. It’s fixed and agreed before you sign.

03

Optional Add-Ons

Extra tools you can add when you’re ready. Copy trading, PAMM and MAM, additional payment gateways, advanced risk plugins or a second platform. None of these are required at launch.

MT4 Grey Label Pricing Compared With MT5

Brokers often assume MT4 grey label pricing is very different from MT5. In practice, the gap between the two platforms is usually modest.

What moves the price more is everything around the platform. The number of symbols, how many groups you need, which integrations you add and whether you want both platforms running together.

01

When MT4 Is the Better Value

If your traders are mainly forex focused and rely on MT4 indicators and EAs, an MT4 grey label keeps them happy and keeps your setup simple.

02

When MT5 Is Worth It

If you plan to offer indices, commodities, stocks or crypto CFDs, MT5 handles everything in one place. Paying for MT5 now usually works out cheaper than migrating later.

05 / Explore the detail

Is There Such a Thing as a Cheap Forex Grey Label?

Yes and no. You’ll find providers advertising a cheap forex grey label at prices that look almost too good. Sometimes they’re genuine. More often, the low headline figure is followed by extra charges for reports, plugins, configuration changes or support hours.

01

What to Check Before You Sign

Ask exactly what the monthly fee covers. Ask whether routine changes cost extra. Ask what support looks like at 2am during a market spike. And ask for every fee in writing. A cheap offer that can’t answer these clearly usually ends up costing more.

02

How We Keep Costs Low Without Cutting Corners

We price our packages so new grey label brokers can afford them, and we only include what you’ll actually use. Start lean, then add tools as your volumes grow. Your costs scale with your revenue rather than running ahead of it.

Costs Outside Your Grey Label

To budget properly, you’ll also need to plan for third-party costs. Licensing and company registration, legal and compliance advice, liquidity provider deposits or fees, payment processor charges and marketing. These don’t come from us, but we’d rather you know about them now.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I start on a smaller package and upgrade later?

Yes. Many brokers start with our Essential package and move up as their client base grows. Upgrades are straightforward and don’t require rebuilding your setup.

Does the monthly fee change as my brokerage grows?

Your fee is fixed for the package you choose. If your client numbers grow well beyond what was planned, or you add new tools, we’ll discuss any change with you before it happens.

Are configuration changes included?

Routine changes like adjusting spreads, adding groups or updating symbols are covered by your monthly fee. Larger projects, such as a new integration, are quoted separately so you can decide.

How do I get an exact quote?

Tell us your target markets, expected client numbers, preferred platform and the features you need. We’ll send a detailed proposal with every cost listed.
