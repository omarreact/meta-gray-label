The context

For a lot of brokerages, partners bring in more clients than any ad campaign ever will. Introducing brokers, affiliates, signal providers and trading educators all have audiences that already trust them. When they recommend you, people listen.

But partners only stay loyal if you treat them well. That means accurate tracking, clear reports and commissions paid on time without arguments. A proper forex IB portal makes all of that automatic, so your partner programme can grow without turning into a spreadsheet nightmare.

Why Your Brokerage Needs Proper IB Tools

Plenty of brokers start by tracking partners manually. It works for a handful of IBs. Then the numbers grow, commission structures get more complicated, and mistakes start creeping in.

01

The Problems With Manual Tracking

Clients get assigned to the wrong partner. Commissions are calculated late or incorrectly. Partners can’t see their own numbers, so they email your team constantly. Disputes eat up time and damage relationships. And the best IBs quietly move to brokers who make their lives easier.

02

What Good Software Fixes

Automated tracking, transparent reporting and accurate commission calculations remove most of that friction. Your partners trust the numbers, your team stops firefighting, and your network grows faster.

02 / Explore the detail

What a Forex IB Portal Gives Your Partners

The IB portal is your partners’ own space, separate from the client trader’s room, where they manage everything about their relationship with you.

01

Unique Referral Links

Each partner gets their own tracking links and codes, so every client they bring in is assigned to them correctly from the moment of registration.

02

Live Performance Data

Partners can see how many clients they’ve referred, how many have funded accounts, and how much those clients are trading.

03

Commission Reports

Clear breakdowns of what they’ve earned, how it was calculated and when it will be paid. No guesswork and no need to email your team.

04

Marketing Materials

Banners, landing pages and promotional content they can use straight away, all carrying your branding.

05

Payout Requests

Partners can request commission withdrawals directly in the portal and track their status.

06

Multi-Level Visibility

If you run a tiered structure, master IBs can see the performance of their sub-IBs too.

03 / Explore the detail

Introducing Broker Software for Your Team

Good introducing broker software isn’t just about the partner-facing side. Your back office needs proper tools to manage the programme.

01

Partner Onboarding and Approval

Review IB applications, verify partner details and approve them through a clear workflow.

02

Client Assignment

Assign clients to partners automatically through referral links, or manually when needed. Move clients between partners with a full record of the change.

03

Flexible Commission Plans

Create different plans for different partners. A new affiliate might get a standard rate, while a high-volume IB gets a custom deal.

04

Fraud and Abuse Checks

Flag suspicious activity like self-referrals, duplicate accounts or unusual trading patterns designed to inflate commissions.

05

Detailed Reporting

See which partners bring in the most funded clients, the most volume and the best long-term value. Use that data to decide where to invest.

04 / Explore the detail

Forex Affiliate Management Made Simpler

Forex affiliate management has its own quirks. Affiliates often care about sign-ups and first deposits more than ongoing trading volume, so they may need a different setup from traditional IBs.

01

CPA Deals

Pay a fixed amount per qualified client, based on rules you set, such as minimum deposit or minimum trading activity.

02

Revenue Share

Pay a percentage of the revenue generated by referred clients over time.

03

Hybrid Deals

Combine an upfront CPA payment with an ongoing revenue share, which suits many larger affiliates.

04

Campaign Tracking

Track which campaigns, landing pages or channels bring in the best clients, so affiliates can focus their efforts and you can reward what works.

05 / Explore the detail

How the IB Commission System Works

The IB commission system is the engine behind your whole partner programme. When it’s accurate and transparent, partners trust you. When it isn’t, nothing else matters much.

01

Commission Types

Pay per lot traded, a share of spread, a share of commission, a fixed amount per trade, or a combination. Rates can vary by symbol, account type or partner.

02

Multi-Tier Structures

Reward master IBs for clients brought in by their sub-IBs, with clear rules on how commissions are split between levels.

03

Automatic Calculation

Commissions are calculated from real trading data synced from MetaTrader. No manual entry and no spreadsheets.

04

Scheduled Payouts

Pay commissions daily, weekly or monthly, either automatically or after approval. Payouts can go to a trading account or through your payment methods.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I set different commission rates for different partners?

Yes. You can create separate plans for different partners, account types or symbols, and set custom deals for your top IBs.

Does the system support multi-level IB structures?

Yes. Master IBs can earn from sub-IBs below them, with clear rules for how commissions are split at each level.

Can partners see their reports in real time?

Yes. Partners can log in to their portal at any time to see referred clients, trading volumes and commission earnings.

How are commissions paid?

You choose the payout schedule and method. Commissions can be credited to a trading account or paid out through your connected payment options.
