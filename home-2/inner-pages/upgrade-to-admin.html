The context

A grey label is a great place to grow. You’ve got your brand, your own groups and real control over trading conditions. But at some point, many brokers start to feel the ceiling. Volumes are up, the team is bigger, and the idea of running your own server, under your own licence, starts to sound less like a dream and more like the obvious next step.

If that’s where you are, this page explains what it means to upgrade grey label to admin level, what changes, what it costs in effort and money, and how we help you make the move without disrupting your clients.

01 / Operational scope

What an Admin License Actually Means

On a grey label, you work inside a server that’s licensed and controlled by your provider. You have manager access, but the deepest settings, server configuration and licence ownership stay with them.

With an admin licence, the server is yours. You hold the licence, you get full administrator access and you decide how every part of the environment is configured. Plugins, server settings, data feeds, gateways, backups, all of it.

01

The Practical Difference

On a grey label, you run your business on someone else’s platform. With an admin licence, the platform itself belongs to you. That brings more freedom, more responsibility and a stronger position when talking to banks, regulators and investors.

02 / Operational scope

Signs You’re Ready to Upgrade

Not every broker should make this move, and certainly not too early. Here are the signals that usually mean the timing is right.

01

Your Volumes Justify the Cost

When your monthly grey label fees, plus the revenue you’re leaving on the table, start to approach what owning your own server would cost, the maths begins to favour an upgrade.

02

You Need Deeper Customisation

Maybe you want plugins your provider can’t run, custom server-side logic, or integrations with tools that need administrator access. Those are hard to achieve on a grey label.

03

Regulators or Partners Expect It

Some licensing authorities, banking partners and institutional clients prefer, or require, that a broker owns its trading infrastructure. An admin licence can make those conversations much easier.

04

You Have the Team to Support It

Running your own server means taking on more technical responsibility. If you’ve got, or can hire, people who understand MetaTrader administration, you’re in a much better position to upgrade.

03 / Operational scope

MT4 Admin License Upgrade: How It Works

An MT4 admin license upgrade sounds complicated, and done badly, it can be. Done properly, your traders barely notice anything happened.

01

Planning

We start by mapping your current setup. Groups, symbols, trading conditions, plugins, CRM connections, payment flows and liquidity routes. Nothing gets moved until we know exactly what’s there.

02

Licensing and Server Setup

We guide you through acquiring your own licence and set up your new server environment, configured to match what you already run, plus any improvements you’ve been wanting.

03

Migration

Client accounts, history, balances and open positions are moved across, usually over a weekend when markets are closed. We test everything thoroughly before reopening.

04

Go Live and Handover

Your team gets full administrator access and training on what’s new. Our support continues afterwards, so you’re never left managing a new server alone.

04 / Operational scope

Grow From Grey Label to White Label: Clearing Up the Terms

You may see the phrase grow from grey label to white label and wonder if it’s backwards. It depends on who’s talking.

In everyday broker conversations, a white label usually means the lightest entry option, a branded platform on someone else’s server with limited control. That’s how we use it elsewhere on this site.

But MetaQuotes, the company behind MetaTrader, uses “White Label” to describe its full branded server licence. In that sense, growing from a grey label to a white label means moving to your own licensed server, which is exactly what this page is about.

01

Why the Confusion Matters

If a provider offers you a “white label upgrade,” ask exactly what you’ll own. Is it a full licence in your company’s name with administrator access? Or a branded platform on their server with less control than you have now? The answer changes everything.

05 / Operational scope

What You Gain After Upgrading

Full control over server configuration and plugins. Ownership of your licence and trading infrastructure. Freedom to choose your own hosting, data centres and technology partners. A stronger profile with regulators, banks and investors. And no ceiling on how far you can customise your brokerage.

01

What You Take On

More responsibility for uptime, security and maintenance. Higher upfront licence costs. A need for in-house or outsourced technical expertise. We’ll be honest about all of this, because an upgrade only makes sense if you’re ready for both sides of it.

06 / Operational scope

How We Support Your Upgrade

Because we already know your grey label setup inside out, we’re well placed to handle the move. We plan the migration, set up and configure your new server, transfer your clients and data, and stay on as your technical partner afterwards if you’d like us to.

Some clients keep us on for full server management. Others take most of it in-house and use us for specialist support. Either works.

Answers, without the ambiguity

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does an upgrade to an admin licence take?

It depends on the licensing process and the size of your setup. The migration itself is usually done over a weekend, but planning and licence approval can take several weeks. We’ll give you a realistic timeline at the start.

Will my clients notice the change?

If it’s planned well, very little. Accounts, balances and history move across, and clients log in to a new server address. We help you communicate the change clearly so nobody is caught off guard.

Can I upgrade from MT5 as well as MT4?

Yes. While this page focuses on the MT4 admin licence upgrade, we support the same move for MT5 brokers, or for brokers running both platforms.

Do I need my own technical team after upgrading?

You’ll need someone responsible for server administration. That can be an in-house team, us, or a mix of both. We’ll help you decide what makes sense for your size.
