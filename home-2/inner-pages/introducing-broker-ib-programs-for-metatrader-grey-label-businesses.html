Introducing Broker (IB) Programs for MetaTrader Grey Label Businesses

Practical perspectives on brokerage technology and operations.

5 min read · Meta Grey Label

The context

Ask any established broker where their best clients came from, and there’s a good chance the answer is a person, not an ad. A trading educator with a loyal following. A local partner who runs workshops. A money manager who brought their investors along. That’s the introducing broker model, and for many brokerages it does more for growth than any paid campaign.

If you run a MetaTrader grey label, you’re in a good position to build a strong forex ib program, because you control the server, the account groups and the data that make IB rewards work. Here’s how to do it well.

What an IB Actually Does

An introducing broker refers clients to your brokerage in exchange for a share of the revenue those clients generate. They don’t hold client funds or execute trades. That stays with you. Their job is to find traders, build trust and point them your way.

IBs come in many shapes:

Trading educators and course creators

Signal providers and analysts

Local agents who handle relationships in a specific country

Affiliates who run websites, YouTube channels or social accounts

Money managers bringing their investor base

Each brings a different kind of client, and each tends to want a slightly different deal.

02 / Explore the detail

Why Grey Label Makes IB Programmes Easier to Run

On a white label, your IB options are often limited by how the host sets up groups and reporting. With a grey label, you have admin access to your own server, so you can design your programme around your business instead of someone else’s setup.

A well-built metatrader grey label ib structure lets you:

Create dedicated account groups for each IB, with their own spreads or commissions

Track every trade back to the IB who referred the client

Pull accurate volume and revenue data straight from your own server

Adjust conditions for top-performing partners without affecting other clients

That flexibility matters. Good IBs compare brokers carefully, and the ability to offer tailored terms is often what wins them over.

03 / Explore the detail

Common IB Reward Structures

There’s no single right way to pay IBs. Most brokers use one of these models, or a mix.

01

Rebates per Lot

The IB earns a fixed amount for every lot their clients trade. It’s simple and transparent, and it’s popular with IBs whose clients trade actively.

02

Spread Markups

The IB’s clients trade on slightly wider spreads, and the IB keeps the difference. This gives the IB some control over their earnings, but it needs careful handling so clients still get competitive pricing.

03

Revenue Share

The IB receives a percentage of the net revenue their clients generate. This lines up the IB’s interests with yours, but it requires clear, trustworthy reporting, because IBs will want to see exactly how revenue is worked out.

04

CPA (Cost per Acquisition)

A one-off payment for each client who meets set conditions, such as a minimum deposit and trading volume. This is more common with affiliates than traditional IBs, and it needs strong fraud controls.

05

Multi-Level Structures

IBs can recruit sub-IBs and earn a share of their commissions. This can grow your network quickly, but it gets complicated fast. Keep the number of levels manageable and the rules very clear.

04 / Explore the detail

The Tools You Need

Running an IB programme on spreadsheets works for about three partners. After that, it becomes a source of errors and arguments.

At minimum, you’ll want:

An IB module in your CRM that tracks referrals, works out commissions and handles multiple levels

Real-time sync with your MetaTrader server so trade data feeds into commission calculations automatically

An IB portal where partners can see their clients, volumes and earnings without emailing your team

Tracking links and promo materials that make it easy for IBs to send traffic your way

Automated payouts on a fixed schedule

The IB portal especially is worth getting right. Partners who can see their numbers clearly trust you more and complain less.

05 / Explore the detail

Pay Accurately, Pay on Time

This sounds obvious, but it’s where many IB programmes fall apart. IBs talk to each other. A broker who pays late or gets calculations wrong will hear about it, and so will every other IB in that network.

Set a clear payout schedule and stick to it. Make sure commission calculations match what IBs see in their portal. If a mistake happens, fix it fast and explain what went wrong. Your reputation with partners is worth far more than the cost of a correction.

06 / Explore the detail

Protect Yourself From Abuse

Most IBs are honest, but any reward system attracts a few people looking to game it. Common problems include:

IBs trading heavily on their own linked accounts just to generate rebates

Clients and IBs coordinating trades that cancel each other out across accounts

Fake or duplicate accounts set up to trigger CPA payments

Churning, where clients are pushed to overtrade to boost commissions

With a grey label, you can watch for these patterns directly on your server. Risk tools that flag linked accounts and unusual trading behaviour are worth having before your programme grows. Clear terms that allow you to hold or cancel payouts for abuse help too.

07 / Explore the detail

Treat IBs as Partners, Not Lead Sources

The brokers with the strongest IB networks don’t just send a contract and a tracking link. They invest in the relationship.

That might mean a dedicated account manager for top partners, co-branded webinars or materials, early access to new account types or features, or regular calls to hear what their clients are asking for. IBs are close to the market. They often know what traders want before your marketing team does.

08 / Explore the detail

Getting Started

If you’re building a programme from scratch, keep the first version simple:

One or two reward structures you can explain in a sentence

A clean IB portal with accurate, real-time data

A fixed monthly or weekly payout schedule

Clear terms covering abuse and payout conditions

Add complexity only once the basics run smoothly and you understand which partners are actually bringing in quality clients.

09 / Explore the detail

Conclusion

A strong forex ib program can become your most dependable growth channel, as long as it’s built on accurate data, fair rewards and on-time payouts. Running a metatrader grey label ib setup gives you the control to design the programme your partners actually want, rather than whatever a host allows.
