MetaTrader Grey Label Licensing: What It Includes and Why It Matters

Practical perspectives on brokerage technology and operations.

5 min read · Meta Grey Label

The context

There’s a mix-up that comes up in almost every early conversation with new brokers. Someone hears “license” and assumes it means regulatory approval. It doesn’t. A metatrader grey label license is a technology arrangement. It gives you the right to run your own MetaTrader server under a provider’s master license. It says nothing about whether you’re allowed to offer trading services in a given country.

That distinction matters, and so does knowing exactly what you’re paying for. Here’s a clear look at both.

01 / Explore the detail

Two Very Different Kinds of License

When brokers talk about licensing, they’re usually mixing up two separate things.

The first is your financial or regulatory license. That comes from a regulator, whether that’s a tier-one body or an offshore authority, and it’s what makes your brokerage legal to operate. It covers things like capital requirements, client fund segregation, reporting and compliance staff.

The second is your platform license. That’s your right to use MetaTrader software commercially. MetaQuotes issues full licenses to brokers directly, but they’re costly and hard to get, and new MT4 licenses haven’t been available for years. A grey label is a way to get platform access without going down that route.

You need both. A grey label covers the technology side only. Any reputable provider will ask about your regulatory status before they set you up.

02 / Explore the detail

How a Grey Label License Actually Works

The provider holds a full MetaTrader license from MetaQuotes. Under that license, they deploy a dedicated server instance for your brokerage. It runs separately from other brokers, carries your branding, and you control it at admin level.

So you’re not technically the license holder in MetaQuotes’ eyes. Your provider is. But day to day, you’re running your own server with much of the control a full licensee would have.

That middle ground is where the name comes from. White label is fully shared and fully hosted. A full license is fully yours. Grey sits between the two.

03 / Explore the detail

What’s Typically Included

Packages vary between providers, so always read the agreement line by line. That said, most grey label arrangements cover something like this:

01

The Server and Its Infrastructure

A dedicated MetaTrader server instance, usually MT5

Hosting in a data centre of your choice, or one the provider recommends

A backup server for failover

Ongoing maintenance, security patches and platform updates from MetaQuotes

02

Terminals and Access

Administrator terminal, giving you full control of server settings

Manager terminals for your dealing desk, support and back office teams

Branded client terminals for desktop, web, iOS and Android

Your own server name, so clients search for your brand when they log in

03

Configuration Rights

This is where the real value sits. With admin access, you can set up symbols, groups, leverage, margin rules, swaps, commissions and execution settings yourself. You choose your liquidity providers. You can install plugins and connect third-party tools without asking the host for permission every time.

04

Support

Most providers include technical support, though the level differs a lot. Some offer round-the-clock monitoring. Others answer emails during office hours. Ask specifically what happens if your server has a problem on a Sunday night before the market opens.

04 / Explore the detail

What’s Usually Not Included

This catches people out. A grey label license generally does not include:

Your regulatory license or legal setup

Liquidity, which you contract separately, even if the provider introduces you

Bridge, CRM, risk tools or other plugins, unless they’re bundled

Payment processing

Your website and marketing

Some providers do offer bundles that bring several of these together. That can be convenient, but check each component’s pricing so you know what you’re really paying for.

05 / Explore the detail

The Real Grey Label License Benefits

So why do so many brokers go this way instead of a white label or a full license? The grey label license benefits come down to a few practical points.

Control without the full price. You get admin-level access and your own server for a fraction of what a full MetaQuotes license costs. For most mid-sized brokers, the full license simply isn’t worth it.

Freedom over liquidity and execution. You aren’t tied to the host’s liquidity or their way of handling orders. You can run A-book, B-book or hybrid on your own terms, and switch liquidity providers if pricing or fills get worse.

Your data stays separate. Client and trade data sit on your own server instance, not on a shared server with other brokers. Regulators and institutional partners tend to look more kindly on that.

Room to grow. As your volume increases, you can add plugins, adjust your setup and scale without migrating to a new platform. Many brokers stay on a grey label for years.

Faster launch than a full license. Getting a full license approved and built can take months. A grey label can be running in days once your paperwork is in order.

06 / Explore the detail

Questions to Ask Before You Sign

Since you’re relying on your provider’s master license, their reliability becomes your reliability. Before committing, it’s worth asking:

How long have they held their MetaQuotes license, and is it in good standing?

What happens to your server and client data if the agreement ends?

Can you migrate to another provider or to your own license later?

What exactly is covered in the monthly fee, and what costs extra?

What uptime do they guarantee, and what happens if they miss it?

The answer to the second question in particular tells you a lot. A provider confident in their service won’t make leaving painful.

07 / Explore the detail

Why It Matters for Your Brokerage

Your platform sits at the centre of everything. Clients trade on it, your dealers manage risk through it, and your reports come out of it. Choosing the wrong licensing setup can quietly limit how you price, how you execute and how quickly you can grow.

A metatrader grey label license isn’t the right fit for every brokerage. Very new brokers on tight budgets may be better off starting with a white label. But for brokers who want real control without taking on the cost and complexity of a full license, it’s often the most practical choice available.

08 / Explore the detail

Conclusion

Understand what you’re buying, keep platform licensing and regulatory licensing separate in your head, and read the agreement carefully. Do that, and the grey label license benefits become very clear in how your brokerage runs.
